# Module 3: Design Business Continuity Solutions (15-20%)

This module covers designing backup, disaster recovery, and high availability solutions to ensure business continuity and resilience.

## 📖 Microsoft Official Documentation & Learn

### 1. Design Solutions for Backup and Disaster Recovery
* **Microsoft Learn Path**: [Design solutions for backup and disaster recovery](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/design-solution-for-backup-disaster-recovery/)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Recommend a recovery solution for Azure and hybrid workloads that meets recovery objectives
  * Recommend a backup and recovery solution for compute
  * Recommend a backup and recovery solution for databases
  * Recommend a backup and recovery solution for unstructured data
  * RTO and RPO calculations
  * Azure Backup and Site Recovery design
  * Backup strategies and retention policies

### 2. Design for High Availability
* **Microsoft Learn Path**: [Design for high availability](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/design-solution-for-high-availability/)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Recommend a high availability solution for compute
  * Recommend a high availability solution for relational data
  * Recommend a high availability solution for semi-structured and unstructured data
  * Availability Zones and Sets
  * Load balancing and failover strategies
  * Multi-region and multi-zone design patterns
