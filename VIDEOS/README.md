# Video Training Resources

Visual learning is essential for understanding Azure architecture and design patterns. This section curates the best video resources for AZ-305 preparation, with emphasis on Cloud360 training materials.

## 🌟 Featured: Cloud360 Training

Cloud360 offers comprehensive, professional training that aligns perfectly with the AZ-305 exam objectives.

### Official Cloud360 AZ-305 Course
* **Course Link**: [AZ-305 Azure Solutions Architect Expert - Cloud360 Training](https://www.cloud360.co/az-305-azure-solutions-architect-expert-training)
* **Overview**: This course covers all four domains of the AZ-305 exam, focusing on architectural decision-making and solution design. Designed for experienced Azure professionals transitioning to solutions architect roles.
* **Modules Covered**:
  * Design Identity, Governance, and Monitoring Solutions
  * Design Data Storage Solutions
  * Design Business Continuity Solutions
  * Design Infrastructure Solutions

### Cloud360 YouTube Channel
* **Channel Link**: [Cloud360 Solutions on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Cloud360_Solutions)
* **Content**: The channel features official Azure architecture training, design patterns, and exam preparation tips for multiple Microsoft certifications.
