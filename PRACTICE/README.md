# Practice & Assessment

Testing your architectural knowledge and decision-making skills before taking the actual AZ-305 exam is crucial for success. This section provides links to official practice assessments and community-recommended resources.

## 📝 Official Microsoft Practice Resources

Microsoft provides free resources to help you gauge your readiness for the exam.

### 1. Free Practice Assessment
* **Link**: [Take a free Practice Assessment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/az-305/practice/assessment?assessment-type=practice)
* **Overview**: This official practice assessment tests your architectural knowledge with questions similar to those you will encounter on the actual exam. It provides immediate feedback and explanations for correct and incorrect answers.

### 2. Exam Sandbox
* **Link**: [Explore the Exam Sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo)
* **Overview**: Familiarize yourself with the exam user interface before test day. The sandbox allows you to experience the look and feel of the exam environment, including how to navigate between questions, mark items for review, and submit your exam.

## 💡 Exam Tips

1. **Focus on Decision-Making**: AZ-305 emphasizes architectural choices. Understand the "why" behind each recommendation.
2. **Know the Trade-offs**: Be prepared to evaluate solutions based on cost, performance, security, and availability.
3. **Study Case Studies**: The exam includes scenario-based questions. Practice analyzing complex requirements.
4. **Understand Well-Architected Framework**: Microsoft's Well-Architected Framework is fundamental to the exam.
5. **Review Reference Architectures**: Study Microsoft's reference architectures for common scenarios.
6. **Practice Scenario Analysis**: Develop the ability to quickly assess requirements and recommend appropriate solutions.
7. **Know Service Limits**: Understand the limits and quotas of Azure services to make informed recommendations.
8. **Review the Study Guide**: Regularly review the [official study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-305) to ensure you are comfortable with all "Skills Measured."
